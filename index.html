About me.

I was born in Hong Kong SAR, China. I am an incoming Postdoctoral Fellow at the Chandra Family Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, The University of Texas at Austin. I will join in December 2026 for a one-year appointment, working with Prof. Yaoyao Jia. My research focuses on energy-efficient mixed-signal integrated circuits, biomedical sensing, and hardware–software co-design for edge intelligence.

I received my Ph.D. in Electronic Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in October 2026, advised by Prof. Kong-Pang Pun, completing the program with early graduation. Previously, I received dual bachelor’s degrees from Peking University in IC Design and Integrated System, and Management of Innovation and Entrepreneurship.

I am interested in connecting circuit innovation with practical biomedical and optical applications. Feel free to get in touch about my research.
